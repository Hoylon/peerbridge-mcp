# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, discuss technical tradeoffs
in good faith, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or publication of
another person's private data. Technical criticism should address the work and include
enough evidence for others to reproduce the concern.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels,
and other spaces where someone represents the PeerBridge MCP community.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the same channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close threads,
reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions
should be proportional, documented privately, and avoid disclosing reporter details.

## Attribution

This concise policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
